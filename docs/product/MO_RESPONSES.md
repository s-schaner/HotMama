**Device:** View on phone or tablet, separate camera.

**Live wants:** Score/rally counter + live player stats + opposing team tendencies (server tendencies, hitter tendencies, serve receive stats, what is scoring against us)

**Roster size:** 12+

**Post-practice:** Individual player stats, video clips of key moments, heatmaps, PDF report.
**Report delivery:** Don't care as long as I have it.

**Player ID:** Know OUR players by face/body (with or without jerseys). Opponents by jersey number only.
**Season tracking:** Nice to have, not critical.

**What she liked about old HotMama:**
- Live statting for matches — objective real-time read on what's working
- Rotation breakdown (side-out %, which rotations get stuck and why)
- Manual score adjustment (refs miss calls)
- Opportunity mapping — where ball drops on D/serve receive, where to attack

**What current software gets wrong:**
- Gets stats WRONG often, requires coach correction
- Does NOT live stat

**Dream feature:** Live stats with analysis of which aspects are most impacting score outcome
